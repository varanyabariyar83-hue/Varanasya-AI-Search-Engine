Dental implants

How an implant is built

An implant has three parts. They go in from the bone upward.

  1. Fixture

    A titanium implant is placed in the jawbone. It acts as the new root and is given time to bond with the bone.

  2. Abutment

    A small connector is fixed to the post and rises through the gum.

  3. Crown

    A crown made to match your neighbouring teeth is fitted on top. This is the part you see and chew with.
